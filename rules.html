Design rules

Event gallery · Wireframe

MadUX is an English-speaking design community in Madrid. These rules define a shared visual identity for event covers and Instagram Stories. They apply to any design tool or publishing workflow.

Keep fixed

Element Rule
Formats Eventbrite 2160 × 1080 px (2:1), Instagram Stories 1080 × 1920 px (9:16). Use the same type, colours, artwork and event details in both.
Title Use uppercase Syne ExtraBold with slightly tight tracking. Run the block to the top, bottom and left trim edges. Break long words at a readable point and keep short words intact. Adjust line lengths to fill the left field, up to 51.5% of the canvas.
Right column Start at 56.3% of the width and end at 95.4%. Stack the full logo, the event hook, the date-and-illustration band and the community footer. Use whitespace to separate rows. Give the logistics the left third of the band and the artwork the other two thirds, and align them along their bottom edge.
Masthead Use the complete MadUX logo, including the bear and lettering. Place it on its own at the left edge of the right column, in white or black for contrast.
Logistics Set the day, month, year and Madrid local start time in Space Mono. Give the day the event accent and use Inter for the venue and optional short address. Keep the place name readable without the address.
Supporting type Use Inter for one concise event hook, and uppercase Space Mono for the footer. Keep the last phrase of the hook together.
Footer Use English-speaking design community in Madrid and the seven-star Madrid signature in their fixed positions.

Change by event

Change the event title, logistics and copy, the palette and the illustration. Choose one ground, one readable ink and one strong accent, with extra shades inside the illustration where needed. Build the image around one recognisable gesture. Use repetition, cut forms, halftones or optical lines to connect it to the rest of the family.

Adapt to Stories

Stack the title panel above the editorial grid. Keep the title flush to the left edge of its panel and reserve space at the top and bottom for Instagram’s interface. Keep date, time and venue beside the same illustration. Use the same full logo, hook, event details and community footer.

Source and release checks

The logistics placement follows the original Lightning Talks poster’s date-and-time line and venue beneath it, the Beautiful Disasters when-and-where group, and the accent-coloured details on Systems & Soul. See the MadUX community presentation, slides 6–9.

The published MadUX poster examples use past editions. The Spring Portfolio Review and Design Walk details come from the organiser’s Eventbrite listings. The New Year Kick-Off venue is unverified. Portfolio Review listing · Design Walk listing

Write the hook around what people will do or learn. Before publishing a new edition, confirm the date, local start time and venue. Use the event listing for the full schedule, address and booking details. Check the placement of key information on both formats before export.
